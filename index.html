Reachy Mini · OpenAI Chat (Open)

Talk with your Reachy Mini using your own OpenAI Realtime key. Open edition: no logging — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

What it does

  • 🎙️ Live voice conversation via the OpenAI Realtime API
  • 💫 Ambient movement — gentle "breathing" and idle motion
  • 🗣️ Speech-reactive head & antenna wobble while talking
  • 😄 Expressive gestures & emotions (nod, tilt, wiggle, dance…)
  • 👀 Camera vision — ask "what do you see?" and it looks & tells you
  • 🙂 Optional face tracking so it follows you around
  • 🔒 No logging — conversations are never saved or uploaded

Setup

  1. Install this app on your robot from the Reachy Mini dashboard.
  2. Add your OPENAI_API_KEY to a .env file (see .env.example).
  3. Start the app and say hello!
